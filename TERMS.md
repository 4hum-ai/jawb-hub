operates jawb and jawb Hub ("we"), and anyone who submits or maintains a
listing ("you", the publisher). They also tell people who install a listed
extension what a listing means. Opening a listing pull request means you
accept them.

## 1. What a listing is

- jawb Hub is a directory. A listing records that the exact bytes named by
  its `artifact_sha256` passed our automated checks when they ran.
- **A listing is not a review, an endorsement, a certification, a signature
  or a warranty.** We do not read, test for fitness, or vouch for listed
  code, and we do not host or distribute it. The listing points to where
  you publish it.
- Being listed does not create any partnership, agency or employment
  between you and us.

## 2. Your responsibilities as a publisher

You confirm that:
1. you own the extension's id: the domain it names, or the GitHub account
   in `io.github.<you>.*`. You also have the right to publish the extension
   and everything in it, under the licence you choose.
2. **Your manifest is accurate.** The extension does what its `purpose`
   and tool descriptions say and nothing else. It asks only for the
   origins, sessions, secrets, files and effects it needs, and gives a true
   reason for each.
3. **It is not harmful or deceptive.** It contains no malware and no hidden
   behaviour. It does not collect, send or sell data beyond what its
   manifest declares and its README explains. It does not try to mislead
   the model or the person using it.
4. **It is not on the never-list.** It does none of these:
   - LinkedIn data extraction or outreach;
   - coupon auto-applying at checkout;
   - CAPTCHA solving, or evading rate limits or bot defences;
   - passing a second factor unattended;
   - loosening jawb's policy or safety checks.
5. **It complies with the law, and with the terms of the sites and services
   it is built to use.**
6. **You keep each listed version available** at its source, unchanged,
   for as long as it is listed.
7. **You give a working contact.** You answer security reports, and you fix
   or withdraw a version with a confirmed security problem promptly.

## 3. What we may do

- **Run checks**, and change them as risks change.
- **Refuse a listing**, or remove or revoke a listing or a version, at any
  time and without prior notice, when we believe it breaks these terms,
  puts people at risk, or exposes us to legal risk. When we can, we tell
  you why.
- **Security takedowns.** Our target is to remove a listing within 24 hours
  of confirming a security report. Once jawb's signed index carries
  third-party listings, we also revoke the version, so installed copies
  switch off.
- **Name and namespace disputes.** Proof of the domain decides. A trademark
  complaint may suspend a listing while it is resolved.
- **Show listing data** (id, purpose, publisher, owners, homepage, versions,
  tools) on jawb.app and in jawb's software.
